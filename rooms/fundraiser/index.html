Proposal to start a fundraiser for Jeff Bezos

Despite how much Jeff Bezos's work has contributed to the well-being of humankind, in 2007, he reportedly paid $0 in federal income taxes.

ZERO DOLLARS.

How is a man supposed to pay his bills under these circumstances? How many yachts will he have to sell?

We cannot allow our billionaires to earn no taxable income. They are human beings, too! They have mortgages, private jets, and superyachts to maintain. Without our help, they could starve to death!

Please, show your solidarity and donate to Jeff Bezos today.

Can't afford to donate? No problem! Simply stop supporting your local small business. Every little sacrifice helps bring us one step closer to ensuring that no billionaire ever has to experience financial hardship.

Every donation counts. Today, it's Jeff Bezos. Tomorrow, it could be you.

Just imagine if you ever find yourself facing the unimaginable despair of having to sell your THIRD-largest yacht, wouldn't you want someone to help you?

Together, we can build a world where no billionaire has to face that kind of suffering alone.

Donate today. Because the wealthiest among us deserve the help.

UPDATE: Happy Ending - The U.S.Government stepped in and in 2011 Jeff Bezos reportedly received a $4,000 tax credit for his children. The world is healing.
